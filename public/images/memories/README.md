# Personal Photographs for Ananya's Scrapbook

Place your photos in this folder with the following filenames:
- memory-01.webp (or .jpg / .png)
- memory-02.webp
- memory-03.webp
- memory-04.webp
- memory-05.webp
- memory-06.webp

After copying your photos here:
1. Open `src/data/memories.js`
2. Update the `type: "personal"` property
3. Customize captions and dates to reflect your real memories!
